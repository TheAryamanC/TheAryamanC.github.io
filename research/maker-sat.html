MAKER-SAT

MAKER is a framework that breaks a huge task down into the smallest possible single steps, giving each step to a tiny, focused AI agent. Multiple agents vote on every single move, while a filter automatically throws out any messy or confused answers before they are counted. This way, the system is able to solve extremely long and complex problems without making any mistakes.
